ITEMS WITH NO WAY TO GET ONE - 14 October 2026
================================================================================

Asked for after a correct hunch: "I'm almost positive the seed box, arkan blade and most slayer
helmets aren't obtainable." All three are right.

HOW TO GET THIS LIST YOURSELF

    cd content && python tools/obtainable.py --soft

--soft is the flag that matters. The bare run prints only counts for the big bucket; --all prints
the 377 cache's own 1,996 unreachable objs instead, which is a different and much less interesting
list. I read --all first and concluded the tool had missed the seed box and the Arkan blade. It had
not - they were in --soft all along, and the mistake was mine.

WHAT THE TOOL IS AND IS NOT SURE OF

obtainable.py splits no-source objs three ways:

  NOTHING ANYWHERE MENTIONS THESE (4)   nothing names them at all. Four Lunar Diplomacy items.
  BUILT WITH NO SOURCE ON PURPOSE (85)  declared in slayerhelmspec.json, petspec.json and
                                        nosourcespec.json - including ELEVEN of the fourteen slayer
                                        helmet colours, whose head-dropping monsters are not in this
                                        era. Only black, green and red can be made. So "most slayer
                                        helmets are not obtainable" is true, and it is a decision
                                        rather than a bug.
  MENTIONED, BUT NEVER GIVEN OUT (206)  something names them, so a route MAY exist through a call
                                        the scanner cannot follow. This is the bucket below, and it
                                        is a mix.

The tool calls that last bucket "worth a glance, not a bug list", and that hedge is doing real work
in both directions. Hand-checked:

  REACHABLE - the scanner simply cannot follow how
    trident_of_the_seas      inv_setslot in trident.rs2, after the Kraken's own drop
    abyssal_tentacle         inv_setslot in abyssal_tentacle.rs2
    dragonfire_shield        inv_setslot in dragonfire_shield.rs2, built from the visage
    armadyl_godsword         return(armadyl_godsword) out of godsword.rs2, joining a hilt
    barrows_teleport         ~barrows_reward_add from the Barrows chest

  GENUINELY UNOBTAINABLE - built, wired, and no route in
    seed_box     a whole storage system: fill, empty, check, open, close, destroy. The ONLY thing
                 that hands one over is ~storage_swap(seed_box, ..) on the OPEN box - so you need a
                 seed box to get a seed box. A closed loop is not a source.
    arkan_blade  a level 60 attack requirement and a special attack in BOTH the PvM and PvP tables,
                 and nothing anywhere gives one.
    mith_grapple gwd_encampments.rs2 checks whether you are carrying one. Nothing sells, drops or
                 makes one.
    xerician_hat a level 20 magic and defence gate, and nothing else at all.
    granite_body in poh_store.enum, which is the COSTUME ROOM'S STORAGE list - that says you may
                 keep one, not that you may get one. Same for granite_helm.

The pattern worth noticing: a cluster of equipment has stats, level requirements and in one case a
special attack written for it, and no source. Those are not cache leftovers; somebody built them.

WHAT TO DO WITH THE REST

The 206 below are grouped by the config that defines them, with the scripts that name each one.
Separating "reachable" from "hole" is a per-item read - the two lists above took about a dozen
greps. Start where it matters: equipment and rewards, not the hundred-odd board game pieces, which
dominate by count and are placed by their own minigame.
