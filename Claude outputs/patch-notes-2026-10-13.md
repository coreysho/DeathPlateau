# Death Plateau — Patch Notes

**7 – 13 October**

A big one. Pest Control, a new Wilderness currency with a shop behind it, the vote shop,
the elite achievement diary rewards, God Wars rebuilt to Old School's own spawns, the
dragonfire shield, and a long tail of fixes from your reports.

---

## New Content

### Pest Control — solo
The island, the lander, the portals, the Void Knights and all 54 pests were in the cache
with full art and animations and not one combat stat. They have them now, and the
minigame is live as a **single-player** game: one player against a clock, no lobby and no
queue, because the five-player version is content a server this size would never see.
Commendation points and the Void Knight equipment are in.

Deliberately not included: the three landers by combat level (the pests' tier comes off
your combat level instead), the barricades, the spinners repairing portals, and the
activity requirement.

### Death tokens and the Undertaker
A new currency, and the only place it comes from is **a kill that counts towards your
Slayer task and happens in the Wilderness**. Both halves — the kill has to be one the task
itself accepted. The rate rises with depth: roughly 0.17 tokens a kill at the ditch and
0.49 past level 30, so a 150-kill task done deep is worth about three times one done at
the edge.

The Undertaker takes them, and a one-armed veteran nearby will tell you what they are.
On his shelf:

- **The Undertaker's outfit** — hat, coat, trousers, boots and cane. Wearing the four
  armour pieces pays **10% more Death tokens**; the cane is pure look, because it takes
  the weapon hand.
- **The Undertaker's cape**, 400 tokens — and an **Undertaker max cape**, made by
  combining it with a Max cape. Black with the gold trim kept, hood to match. The first
  Max cape variant Old School does not have.
- **The Gravedigger kit**, 800 tokens — a slayer helmet in the Undertaker's black and bone.
- **Nine abyssal whip colours** — volcanic, frozen, venom, gilded, shadow, bloodbark,
  tempest, bone, and a gravedigger whip in the Undertaker's own colours. Reversible: the
  kit comes back off.

Everything on that shelf is staked the way Wilderness gear should be — none of it is one
of the three items an unskulled player keeps, and a quarter of its price goes to whoever
kills you wearing it.

### The vote shop
Two things, and they are deliberately not the same thing.

- **Vote tickets** are a tradeable, stackable item. If you do not want anything in the
  shop, sell them. If you do, buy more than you voted for.
- **Vote points** are what a redeemed ticket becomes, one for one. No trade, no drop, no
  death loss, and no way back to a ticket. Redeeming asks before it does a whole stack,
  and there is a **Redeem X**.

Your point balance sits on the **Player Statistics** tab. The **Vote clerk** stands by the
Edgeville bank and sells the rewards below.

arrivals are measured against the game's own collision map, so none of them drops you in
a hedge.

### God Wars Dungeon
Largely rebuilt from your reports.

- **298 spawns moved to Old School's own coordinates**, replacing 147 that had been placed
  by eye.
- **All four encampments are garrisoned.** They held a general and three bodyguards each in
  rooms of 250 to 340 tiles; they now hold what Old School puts in them, Zamorak's
  minions included.
- **Nine obstacle animations** that were all playing the same stoop-and-pick-up: heaving the
  boulder, squeezing through the crack both ways, tying and climbing the rope, the grapple
  at Armadyl's pillar, Bandos' gong, Zamorak's ice bridge and Saradomin's rocks. Each has
  its own now.
- The shaft's black hole is a plain loc, the unreachable wolves are reachable, and three
  one-way trips behave like one-way trips.

### The dragonfire shield
The **draconic visage** and the **dragonfire shield**, imported from the rev 474 cache that
first held them. Old School's stats, reaching 70/75/72/72 by +1 a charge at runtime, and
the **Activate** special: one charge, up to 25 damage at 10 tiles, on about a one minute
fifty-six second cooldown, with the accuracy built from your Defence against the target's
Magic defence. On the collection log, and every source that drops a visage logs it.

### Ankou and Lava Dragon Isle
Both in the Wilderness, both at Old School's coordinates, both in 2006's art — the ankou
straight out of the 474 cache, which turns out to be the same model Old School uses today.
Lava Dragon Isle has the red dragons out and the lava dragons in, with their full drop
table and the dragon bones that are the reason to go.

**Duradel assigns both now.** Ankou are a plain task; lava dragons are behind a new
unlock, **Playing with Fire**, at 100 Slayer points.

### Graceful recolours
Marks of grace have had nowhere to go since the rooftops went in. **Grace sells nine
recolour kits at 50 marks each**, one named after each rooftop course — because marks only
drop on those rooftops, so the shelf and the currency describe each other. Reversible.

### The Ectofuntus
Entirely unscripted until now, and that was four reports at once. Worship, the trapdoor,
the four-level slime cavern, the buckets, the bonemeal and the Prayer experience — all of
it live. The **ectophial** has its real pouring animation and comes back full.

### A Wilderness fishing spot
**Sea turtle (79) and manta ray (81)** can be harpooned from a Wilderness spot now, not
only won from the Fishing Trawler. It is a sidegrade and not a replacement: the catch
rates are exactly the shark's, so the spot pays more because the fish are worth more, not
because the rolls are kinder. The water is west of the dragons.

### Fighting TzTok-Jad again
Once you have beaten him, the cave entrance offers a choice: **start from wave 1, or fight
TzTok-Jad only.** A shortcut clear pays the cape, the pet roll and Jad's own 4,000 Tokkul —
and nothing for the 62 waves it skipped.

**The Fight Cave is multi-combat now**, which it always should have been. Jad's four
Yt-HurKot walk up and melee, and in a single-way area a healer touching you is what stopped
you attacking anything else — so the healers could not be killed and Jad was being healed
40 a round by monsters that were effectively invulnerable. It is a harder cave and a
correct one.

## Skills and Quality of Life

- **Farming growth times: the long tail is halved.** Every family whose longest crop took
  more than three hours now takes half as long — trees (oak 2h40 to 1h20, through to magic
  8h to 4h), fruit trees 16h to 8h, calquat 21h20 to 10h40, cactus 9h20 to 4h40, mushroom
  4h to 2h, belladonna 5h20 to 2h40, spirit tree 64h to 32h. **Flowers, allotments, hops,
  herbs and bushes are unchanged**, so the 80-minute farm run is exactly as it was. Produce
  regrowth is unchanged too: a grown fruit tree still puts a fruit back every 40 minutes.
- **Alchemy works on the move.** Casting was discarding your walk clicks for the next few
  ticks, so a run across Varrock became a run that stopped dead.
- **A blade in your backpack cuts a web.** No more equipping the knife, slashing, and
  equipping the shield again.
- **The Agility guide has a Courses page.** Opening it to find out where to train used to
  give you a list of one-off shortcuts, with the nine rooftop courses — the whole of
  Agility from 20 to 99 — as a single unexplained line.
- **The equipment tab's three buttons are a row.** Equipment Stats, Items Kept on Death and
  Call follower now sit evenly spaced in one frame instead of scattered across the tab.
- **119 items that went on at level 1 now ask for the level they should.** Equip
  requirements are written per item here, so a missing one was a line somebody forgot to
  type, and nothing complained.
- **Slayer:** the Rewards window is on every master's **right-click** now, not two chat
  menus deep. Six tasks that had no monster carrying the right tag have one. Three tasks
  that could never be finished can be — the dust devils in particular were assignable by
  three masters with nothing you killed counting, and could be fought at level 1 because the
  level check reads the same tag.
- **The 3rd Age axe chops and the 3rd Age pickaxe mines**, and the gilded axe finally does
  too. A hatchet is wired in three places in this build and these were wired in one.
- **Shops restock properly.** A shop slot holding a non-stackable item, bought down to
  nothing, could never refill — ever, on that world. Grace's kits and the vote shelf were
  both affected.
- **The development world no longer takes your items when you die.** The old rule did the
  exact opposite of what it looked like.
- **Browser client:** the bottom rows are no longer cut off, and the camera turns with the
  mouse wheel.

## Clipping on worn items

**Capes no longer get a wedge cut out of their collar and shoulder.** This is the one that
has been reported most, and it took nine attempts, so it is worth saying what it actually
was.

Every cape carries a drawing priority per face, chosen by whoever made it — the Max cape's
642 faces sit at 1, 4 and 5. The body underneath sits at 2 and 3, and worn armour at 3.
This client has no depth buffer, so those numbers are the whole of the draw order: any cape
face the artist put below 3 was painted over by the torso wherever the two overlapped, and
those faces are the collar and the shoulder. That is why it was always "the same area" on
every cape.

It is not a renumbering, and the first attempt proved it — raising the Max cape's low faces
above the torso fixed the back and turned the chest red, because a collar that beats the
torso from behind also beats it from the front, where the cape is behind you and should
lose. There is no fixed number that is above the torso from one side and below it from the
other, so those faces now go into the one bucket that asks the real depth instead. Every
other face keeps the value its artist chose.

140 capes; 12 needed nothing. Eight are left alone on purpose because an NPC is built from
the same model, which is the mistake that broke the Undertaker during an earlier attempt.

**Still open:** boots can show through the hem of a long cape when the camera is behind and
above you. A fix for that shipped and was rolled back for tearing holes in the capes
themselves, so it is waiting on a better one rather than forgotten.

Thanks for reporting these — the photographs of the exact character it was reported on are
what found the cause. Please keep doing it.
