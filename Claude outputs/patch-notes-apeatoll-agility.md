# Death Plateau — patch notes, 25 October 2026

## New

**The Ape Atoll Agility Course.** The whole course was already standing in the jungle west of Marim
— the stepping stone, the tropical tree, the monkeybars over the cliff, the wall of skulls and the
rope — and not one click of it did anything. It works now.

- **Agility 48** to use it, and a lap is **580 experience**: 40 for the stepping stone, 40 for the
  tropical tree, 40 for the monkeybars, 60 for the skull slope, 100 for the rope, and **300 for
  finishing the lap in order**.
- **The lap runs downhill.** Start on the plateau at the east end, jump the stepping stone, climb the
  tropical tree onto the walkway, swing the monkeybars west over the cliff and drop onto the ledge,
  go up the wall of skulls and down the far side onto the lowland, and finish on the rope swing at
  the south end.
- **The ledge below the monkeybars has one way out**, and that is the skull slope. It is meant to —
  anyone who crossed the bars has the level to climb it.
- **Obstacles can be failed** below 75 Agility, for a little damage and a second try from the tile
  you set out from. At 48 that is about one try in thirteen; from 75 you do not fall at all.
- The **stepping stone** works in either direction, so you can step back across it.
- The **Giant squirrel** can be found on finishing a lap here, at the same rate as the other courses.

---

*Nothing in this release changes existing content. The course was placed on the map and silent; this
is the first time any of it responds.*
