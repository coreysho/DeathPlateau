# Death Plateau — patch notes, 26 October 2026

## Fixes

**The agility courses put you down on tiles that are not there.** Fifteen landings across seven of
the nine courses have been moved onto ground that actually exists. Nothing about how a course plays
has changed - the same obstacles, the same order, the same experience to the point - only where you
are standing when each one lets go of you.

- **Falador's first tightrope** is the clearest of them. Crossing it took you one tile along the
  rope and then slid you seven more, diagonally, over four tiles of open air, and set you down on a
  roof edge with nothing drawn on it. It now walks the length of the rope and stops at the far end,
  which is where the rope ends.
- **Varrock, Falador, Seers' Village, Pollnivneach, Rellekka, Ardougne and Al Kharid** each had
  between one and four obstacles doing a version of the same thing. Hand holds, ledges and
  tightropes are mounted on walls, and the tile chosen for the end of the crossing was often the
  wall itself, which carries no floor at all.
- **Al Kharid's rough wall** was the quietest of them: its landing is commented "the roof over Ellis
  the Tanner's", and there is no roof drawn there - the tile has a height and nothing else.
- **Canifis, Draynor and the Ape Atoll course are unchanged.** Every lap still pays exactly what it
  paid before.

**Two Pollnivneach obstacles are still wrong, and are left alone deliberately.** The market stall
and the second tree set you down in mid-air. The one tile either of them could move to is a market
canopy - something to stand on - and from there the game will not let you click the next obstacle at
all, which would end the lap. Standing in the air with a course that works beats standing on a roof
with a course that stops, so they stay until that market has floor under it.

---

*If you have been running these courses and felt that an obstacle threw you further than it should,
or that you were standing on nothing, you were right.*
