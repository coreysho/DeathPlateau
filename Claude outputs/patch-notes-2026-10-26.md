# Death Plateau — patch notes, 26 October 2026

## Fixes

**The rooftop courses put you on tiles that were not there.** Seventeen landings across six of the
seven courses have been moved onto ground that actually exists.

- **Falador's first tightrope** is the clearest of them. Crossing it took you one tile along the
  rope and then slid you seven more, diagonally, over four tiles of open air, and set you down on a
  blocked roof edge nothing could walk off. It now walks the length of the rope and stops at the far
  end, which is where the rope ends.
- **Varrock, Falador, Seers' Village, Pollnivneach, Rellekka and Ardougne** each had between one and
  four obstacles doing some version of the same thing: hand holds, ledges and tightropes are mounted
  on walls, and the tile picked for the end of the crossing was often the wall itself — blocked,
  with no floor drawn on it, so you were stood in mid-air.
- **Canifis is unchanged**, and no obstacle's experience, level requirement or lap total has moved.
  Every course still pays exactly what it did.

**Two Pollnivneach obstacles are still wrong** and are left alone rather than guessed at: the market
stall and the second tree set you down in mid-air. The lap still works from both — the next obstacle
can be clicked — so this is something you can see rather than something that stops you. They need a
decision about where that market's roofs are meant to be walked, not another sweep.

---

*Nothing about how a course plays has changed: the same obstacles, the same order, the same
experience. What has changed is where you are standing when each one lets go of you.*
