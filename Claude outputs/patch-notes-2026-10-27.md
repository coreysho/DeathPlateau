# Death Plateau — patch notes, 27 October 2026

## New

**Marim's five market stalls can be robbed.** Ape Atoll has had a food stall, a crafting stall, a
general stall, a magic stall and a scimitar stall standing in the village since the island was
built, all five offering **Steal from**, and the click did nothing at all.

- **Food stall** — 5 Thieving, 16 experience, a banana.
- **Crafting stall** — 5 Thieving, 20 experience: a ring, necklace or amulet mould, a chisel or a
  gold bar.
- **General stall** — 5 Thieving, 25 experience: a pot, a hammer or a tinderbox.
- **Magic stall** — 65 Thieving, 90 experience: an air, earth, fire, nature or law rune.
- **Scimitar stall** — 65 Thieving, 210 experience: an iron, steel, mithril or adamant scimitar.

A robbed stall is picked over for a few seconds and then restocks, and **Padulah** — who guards
Marimbo's statue — will come over if he happens to see you at it.
