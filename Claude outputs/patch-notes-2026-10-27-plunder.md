# Death Plateau — patch notes, 27 October 2026 (part two)

## Part 1 of 2

**The Agility Pyramid**

The pyramid in the Kharidian Desert is open. Six storeys, twenty-four obstacles and a solid
gold capstone at the top.

**New**
- **The Agility Pyramid.** Agility 30. Climb the stairs on the south-west corner and work your
  way round and up: stone blocks that tip you across, low walls, narrow ledges, planks, wall
  hangs and gaps to leap. Five stone blocks, four low walls, four ledges, three wall hangs, six
  jumps and two planks, at Old School's experience for each.
- **The capstone.** The top of the pyramid comes off in your hands at the summit. Carry it
  through the doorway up there and you are paid a lap bonus of 300 + 8 × your Agility level, up
  to 1,000. Go through without it and you just come out at the bottom.
- **Simon Templeton** has been standing at the foot of the pyramid since the desert was built
  with nothing to say. He will pay you **10,000 coins** a capstone, and he will tell you how to
  get up there.
- **The Giant squirrel** can be found on the pyramid, rolled when you hand the capstone in.

**Changes**
- Every one of the pyramid's landing tiles and every tile a fall drops you onto has been checked
  against the map. A fall takes you one storey down, not into the stonework.

**Fixes**
- The five stone blocks were buried inside the pyramid, one storey below the walkway each belongs
  to, where nothing could click them and nothing could draw them. They are on the walkways now,
  and they close them — you have to cross.

## Part 2 of 2

**Pyramid Plunder**

Sophanem's second pyramid is open for business, if you can find the right door.

**New**
- **Pyramid Plunder.** Thieving 21. Four anonymous doors on the pyramid north-west of Sophanem;
  one of them is the way in and it changes every twenty-five minutes. Inside, the Guardian mummy
  will start you on a **five-minute** run through eight chambers.
- **Eight rooms**, at 21, 31, 41, 51, 61, 71, 81 and 91 Thieving. Each has a spear trap on the
  way in, thirteen urns, a Grand Gold Chest, a sarcophagus and four tomb doors — one of which is
  the way on. Three wrong doors and the fourth always opens.
- **Nine artefacts** to find, from the ivory comb at 50 coins to the golden statuette at 1,250.
- **The sarcophagus pays Strength**, not Thieving — you are heaving a stone lid off.
- **Watch the urns.** Some have a snake in them. Check before you reach in, or buy a snake charm
  and coax it out.
- **The Grand Gold Chest** is sometimes wired. A trap means scarabs, and no experience — but you
  keep the artefact.
- **The pharaoh's sceptre**, rarely, out of a chest or a sarcophagus. Finding one throws you
  straight out of the pyramid. It teleports you back to the Guardian mummy three times, and he
  will fill it again in exchange for artefacts.
- **Rocky** can be found on the Grand Gold Chest, deeper rooms more often.

**Not built yet, and said plainly:** the two moving Pyramid blocks are not in, and neither is
the empty chamber behind the three wrong doors into the pyramid. Both are noted in the code.
