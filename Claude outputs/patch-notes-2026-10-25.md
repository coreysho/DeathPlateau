# Death Plateau — patch notes, 25 October 2026

## New

**The Ape Atoll Agility Course.** The whole course was already standing in the jungle west of Marim
— the stepping stone, the tropical tree, the monkeybars over the cliff, the wall of skulls and the
rope — and not one click of it did anything. It works now.

- **Agility 48** to use it, and a lap is **580 experience**: 40 for the stepping stone, 40 for the
  tropical tree, 40 for the monkeybars, 60 for the skull slope, 100 for the rope, and **300 for
  finishing the lap in order**.
- **The lap runs downhill.** Start on the plateau at the east end, jump the stepping stone, climb the
  tropical tree onto the walkway, swing the monkeybars west over the cliff and drop onto the ledge,
  go up the wall of skulls and down the far side onto the lowland, and finish on the rope swing at
  the south end.
- **The ledge below the monkeybars has one way out**, and that is the skull slope. It is meant to —
  anyone who crossed the bars has the level to climb it.
- **Obstacles can be failed** below 75 Agility, for a little damage and a second try from the tile
  you set out from. At 48 that is about one try in thirteen; from 75 you do not fall at all.
- The **stepping stone** works in either direction, so you can step back across it.
- The **Giant squirrel** can be found on finishing a lap here, at the same rate as the other courses.

**The Keldagrim minecart system.** The dwarves' cart network was built, staffed and silent: nine Cart
conductors with nothing to say, carts you could click "Ride" on for no result, and the minecart
tickets from The Giant Dwarf with nowhere to use them. It runs now.

- **Three stations**: the Keldagrim depot, the dwarven mines under Ice Mountain, and the east end of
  the passage under White Wolf Mountain. Both outer lines run to Keldagrim and back.
- **Talk to the Cart conductor** at any station and he will sell you a seat for **150 coins** — or
  **75** if you are wearing a Ring of charos(a).
- **Or use a ticket.** Each of the four minecart tickets covers one direction, and the cart itself
  takes it instead of a fare. The ticket The Giant Dwarf gives you is now a free ride rather than a
  keepsake.
- **You need to have started The Giant Dwarf** to ride at all, and the White Wolf line also needs
  the Fishing Contest, since that is what opens the passage it arrives in.
- A cart has been put at the Keldagrim end, which had four platforms and nothing standing at any of
  them.

**Temple Trekking.** Escort a volunteer from Burgh de Rott across Mort Myre to Paterdomus, keeping
them alive on the way. The whole minigame was drawn and staffed and silent: six volunteers standing
invisible in Burgh de Rott, fourteen rooms of swamp with a signposted path out of every one, and
every monster in the cache waiting for something to put it there.

- **Finish In Aid of the Myreque** and the six volunteers appear in Burgh de Rott. Talk to any of
  them to set off.
- **Three roads.** The swamp road is the quietest — about level 80, and you can slip past anything.
  Along the Salve is level 110 or so, and you can only slip past once half of them are down. Through
  the Hollows is level 140 and there is no slipping past anything.
- **Who you take matters, backwards.** The tougher the volunteer, the fewer monsters turn up: the
  retired pair bring three, the militia four, the old man and the child five.
- **Your charge is what the swamp is after.** Anything left standing will go for them while you
  fight, and if they go down the trek is over and you walk out with nothing. The tougher the
  volunteer the more they can take - the retired pair have 70 hitpoints, the militia 50, the old man
  and the child 30 - which is the other half of the trade above.
- **Stock their pack before you set off.** Use food on a volunteer in Burgh de Rott and it goes into
  their pack: up to fifteen items, anything you could eat yourself. Once a mauling has them down to
  half they will take something out of it and get a little back, so a full pack roughly doubles how
  long a soft escort lasts. It empties when the trek does, and they lose it if you log out.
- **Fourteen events, one to a room.** Swamp snakes, ghasts, giant snails, vampyre juvinates and
  shadows; a bridge down over a ditch; a gully to be crossed on a vine rope; and Abidor Crank, who
  will feed you both, steady your hands, and turn anything rotten in your pack into stew.
  - **Ghasts are invisible** without a druid pouch, and a pouch used on one pulls it into the light.
    A ghast still in the fog is the only thing that will go for your charge, so the pouch is how you
    get it off them as much as it is how you kill it.
  - **Juvinates** will not take a scratch from anything but silver.
  - **The bridge** needs three pieces of wood — planks and a hammer, or logs and an axe — and goes
    back together a length at a time. There is no other way over the ditch.
  - **The gully** wants two lengths of vine off a swamp tree, twisted into a rope and thrown over
    the branch that reaches across.
- **Every room has three ways out**: carry on north, slip past, or take the path you came in by and
  give the whole thing up.
- **The Hollows road ends on the water.** Its last leg is a boat across the swamp, and something
  with a head and four tentacles comes up out of it on both sides. There is nowhere to stand but the
  deck, and the boat goes nowhere until the thing is dealt with.
- **Reach Paterdomus** and your charge hands over a reward token — blue off the swamp road, yellow
  off the Salve, red out of the Hollows. Click it to claim what the trek was worth: the harder the
  road and the more you dealt with, the more comes out of it, and tomes of experience are in the
  table.

---

*Nothing in this release changes existing content. The agility course, the cart network and the whole
of Temple Trekking were already drawn on the map and silent; this is the first time any of them
responds.*
